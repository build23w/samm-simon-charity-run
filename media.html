Media and run facts

Source links and facts about Samm Simon’s 251 km Stratford-to-Tobermory charity run, including the May 11–17 dates and dated fundraising update.

Run facts

  • Runner: Samm Simon, Stratford, Ontario.
  • Route: Stratford to the Tobermory pier; 251 km.
  • Dates: May 11–17, 2026.
  • GoFundMe displays CAD $38,660 as of September 21, 2026; donations are paused.
  • Intended equal beneficiaries: local cancer care through London Health Sciences Foundation, Stratford General Hospital's emergency department, and Wellspring Stratford.

The displayed fundraiser total does not confirm disbursement. Read the full run account and view the fundraiser.

Coverage and source material

  • London Health Sciences Foundation: the run begins — May 11, 2026. Source for the start date and intended beneficiaries.
  • StratfordToday: the completed week-long run — May 23, 2026. Reports the May 17 finish.
  • CBC London: Samm's 251 km cancer-care run.
  • CTV London: the Stratford-to-Tobermory run.
  • BlogTO: an Ontario resident's 250-plus-kilometre run.
  • Granthaven: regional cancer-program fundraising.
  • CKNX News Today: supporting local cancer care.

Amounts in older articles reflect their publication dates. Coverage concerns the run; it does not imply endorsement of every project listed on this site.

Corrections and enquiries

Earlier versions of this site listed May 11 as the finish date. The date has been corrected to May 17 using the sources above. For enquiries, email charity@sammsimon.ca. Please request permission before reusing photographs or other media.
