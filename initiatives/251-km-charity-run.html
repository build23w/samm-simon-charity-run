The 251 km Charity Run

What started as one massive personal challenge became Samm Simon's biggest charity effort to date: an epic run for cancer from his front door in Stratford, Ontario to the Tobermory pier, raising over $35,000 for local cancer care.

The challenge

Samm set out to run 251 kilometers from his front door in Stratford, Ontario to the Tobermory pier, through the Bruce Peninsula and past Owen Sound. He finished on May 11, 2026.

Two lifelong friends joined to support and document the journey, traveling in an RV along the route. The run was in support of Samm's wife, who is battling cancer for a third time, and everything raised went to local cancer care rather than to the family.

Simon: “People with cancer didn't choose this. That's what motivates me.”

This page keeps the focus on the run, the community, and the care it funded. Personal medical details stay private.

People noticed. The community responded.

A GoFundMe launched with an initial goal of $4,500. Regional TV, radio, local news, podcasts, and community platforms picked up the story, and supporters pushed the total to over $35,000.

  • CBC London Morning: radio interview about the run and the local impact goal.
  • CTV News London: broadcast feature, plus a video segment.
  • BlogTO and the Stratford Beacon Herald: city and hometown reporting.
  • London Health Sciences Foundation: coverage from one of the beneficiaries.
  • Canada News Today on Apple Podcasts.
  • The Epic Run for Cancer Facebook group and Samm's live thread on Home Renovation Reviews.
  • Sponsors: LF Builders (primary sponsor) and Home Renovation Reviews (live updates).
