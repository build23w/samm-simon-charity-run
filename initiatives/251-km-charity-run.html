The 251 km Charity Run

Stratford to Tobermory on foot, for local cancer care. The GoFundMe opened with a $4,500 goal and passed $35,000, intended for three local organizations.

View fundraiser Watch the story

The run

Samm ran 251 kilometers from his front door in Stratford, Ontario to the pier in Tobermory, up through the Bruce Peninsula and past Owen Sound, and finished on May 17, 2026. Two lifelong friends followed along in an RV to support him and film the trip.

His wife is fighting cancer for the third time. In his words: “People with cancer didn't choose this. That's what motivates me.” None of the money went to the family. All of it went to local cancer care, and the updates stayed respectful on purpose.

Who covered it

  • CBC London Morning had Samm on the radio.
  • CTV News London ran a feature and a TV segment.
  • BlogTO and the Stratford Beacon Herald wrote it up.
  • London Health Sciences Foundation covered it too, and they represent one of the intended beneficiaries.
  • Canada News Today mentioned it on Apple Podcasts.
  • The Epic Run for Cancer Facebook group and Samm's thread on Home Renovation Reviews kept everyone posted along the way.
  • LF Builders was the main sponsor, and Home Renovation Reviews carried the live updates.

Updated September 21, 2026: the run began May 11 and finished May 17. Earlier versions of this site incorrectly listed May 11 as the finish date. Sources: London Health Sciences Foundation and StratfordToday.

Fundraising and intended beneficiaries

A GoFundMe, opened with a $4,500 goal, that passed $35,000. As of September 21, 2026, the fundraiser displays CAD $38,660 and donations are paused. This is the displayed fundraising total, not a confirmation of disbursement. The intended equal beneficiaries are:

  • London Health Sciences Centre, the cancer program
  • Stratford General Hospital, the emergency room
  • Wellspring Stratford, cancer support for patients and families
