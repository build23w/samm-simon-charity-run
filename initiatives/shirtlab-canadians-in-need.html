The run ended. The work carries on.

On May 17, 2026, Samm completed the journey from his front door in Stratford to the pier in Tobermory. The 251 km run raised over $35,000 for local cancer care.

Alongside that, the African crypto charity project has helped raise almost $25,000 for people in need, and that work continues. Shirtlab is the third chapter, with the focus on Canadians who need a helping hand.

How your purchase helps

Every purchase at shirtlab.lol donates $1 to Canadians in need. You can create a custom shirt through Shirtlab and place your order there.

This project is separate from the run’s GoFundMe. The run’s funds continue to support its three local cancer-care organizations. Shirtlab’s $1 donation supports Canadians in need.
