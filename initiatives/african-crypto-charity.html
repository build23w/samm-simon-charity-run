African Crypto Charity Initiative

Almost $25,000 raised for people in need in Africa, using $RENO, with the technical side built by the Psionic Dream IT team. And it isn't over.

Follow Samm on Home Renovation Reviews

What this is

Alongside the run, Samm helped raise almost $25,000 for people in need in Africa. This one didn't go through a GoFundMe. It ran on $RENO, the coin behind the Home Renovation Reviews community, and it took real technical work to pull off.

The Psionic Dream IT team built and ran that side of it. The initiative wouldn't have happened without them.

How the money moved

$RENO is a public token on Solana, and that's the useful part. Anyone can look at the coin on Solscan, the community keeps a public payment ledger on the forum, and the coin's own documentation is open on GitHub. None of it has to be taken on trust.

We're not done

Raising the money was the start. Samm and the team are still helping people there, every day, and this page will keep growing as that work goes on.

Questions about any of it? Email charity@sammsimon.ca. The 251 km run has its own page, and everything Samm has done so far is listed on the home page.
