What it was

Alongside the 251 km run, Samm took part in a separate charity initiative focused on people in Africa. It raised almost $25,000.

This page states what has been confirmed and clearly marks what hasn't. As more details are published, they are added here, never guessed.

How the money was raised

The fundraising was accomplished using $RENO coin, a Solana meme coin.

Details on how contributions were collected and how the funds were delivered will be added once they are confirmed.

Built with the Psionic Dream IT team

The initiative was made possible through the technical work and support of the Psionic Dream IT team. This page credits that work without turning into an advertisement; specifics of the team's contribution will be added if and when they are published.
