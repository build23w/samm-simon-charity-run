What it was

Alongside the 251 km run, Samm took part in a separate charity initiative focused on people in Africa. It raised almost $25,000, and it is still going.

Everything here is verifiable: $RENO is a public Solana token, the community publishes its $RENO payment ledger, and the work continues in the open alongside the Home Renovation Reviews community.

We are not done

Raising almost $25,000 was a milestone, not a finish line. Samm and the team behind this initiative continue to help those in need on a daily basis.

This page will keep growing as that ongoing work is documented. For day-to-day updates, follow Samm's work on Home Renovation Reviews.

How the money was raised

The fundraising was accomplished using $RENO coin, a Solana meme coin and the community reward token of Home Renovation Reviews. It is a public SPL token on the Solana blockchain, so every movement of $RENO can be checked by anyone.

  • $RENO on Solscan: the token, its holders, and its transfers.
  • The public $RENO Payment Ledger: the community's append-only record of treasury drops and transfers, cross-referenced to Solana transactions.
  • $RENO on GitHub: the open-source documentation and white paper behind the token.

The initiative was built and run with the Psionic Dream IT team, raising almost $25,000 for people in need in Africa. That figure keeps moving, because the help hasn't stopped.

Built with the Psionic Dream IT team

The initiative was made possible through the technical work and support of the Psionic Dream IT team, who built and ran the $RENO fundraising behind it. This page credits that work without turning into an advertisement.

Verify it yourself

Where the money goes matters, so this initiative runs on a public blockchain rather than on trust alone.

  • The token is public. $RENO is a Solana SPL token; its supply, holders, and transfers are visible to anyone on Solscan.
  • The ledger is public. The community keeps an append-only $RENO Payment Ledger of treasury drops and transfers, each cross-referenced to a Solana transaction.
  • The code is public. The token's documentation and white paper live in the open-source renovation.reviews repository.
