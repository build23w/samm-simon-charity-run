About Samm Simon

Samm Simon of Stratford, Ontario, organized the 251 km run for local cancer care. Learn about the run, his projects and where to follow him.

The run that brought people together

Samm Simon is from Stratford, Ontario. He organized a 251 km run from Stratford to the Tobermory pier in support of local cancer care. The run began May 11 and finished May 17, 2026.

The full run story preserves the route, community support and fundraising details. The dates are supported by London Health Sciences Foundation and StratfordToday.

Projects and community

This site also documents an African crypto charity initiative and the Shirtlab initiative for Canadians in need. Explore the projects and their connections.

LF Builders sponsored the run, and Home Renovation Reviews hosted community updates. Those relationships are part of the run's story.
