The 251 km run started it. The mission continues.

Samm completed his 251 km epic run for cancer on May 11, 2026: from his front door in Stratford, Ontario to the Tobermory pier, in support of his wife, who is battling cancer for a third time. The community responded, and the run raised over $35,000 for local cancer care. It became Samm's biggest charity effort to date, and this site is now the permanent home for that story and for the charity and community initiatives that follow it.

The run that started it

The 251 km Charity Run

What started as one massive personal challenge became Samm's biggest charity effort to date. Compassion first: the goal was tangible support for people who never chose a cancer diagnosis.

One day. One long road.

On May 11, 2026, Samm completed 251 kilometers - from his front door in Stratford, Ontario to the Tobermory pier, through the Bruce Peninsula.

Two lifelong friends joined to support and document the journey, traveling in an RV along the route.

100% to three charities

  • London Health Sciences Centre – Cancer Program
  • Stratford General Hospital (ER)
  • Wellspring Stratford Cancer Centre

A GoFundMe launched with an initial goal of $4,500 and has now raised over $35,000. Every dollar is split evenly between these three organizations.

Simon: “People with cancer didn't choose this. That's what motivates me.”

People noticed. The community responded.

Regional TV, radio, local news, and podcasts picked up the story, and supporters well beyond Samm's own network pushed the GoFundMe from its $4,500 starting goal to over $35,000.
