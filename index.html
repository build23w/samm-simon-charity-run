The 251 km Charity Run

Compassion first. The goal is tangible support - for people who never chose a cancer diagnosis.

One day. One long road.

On May 11, 2026, Samm completed 251 kilometers - from his front door in Stratford, Ontario to the Tobermory pier, through the Bruce Peninsula.

Two lifelong friends joined to support and document the journey, traveling in an RV along the route.

100% to three charities

  • London Health Sciences Centre – Cancer Program
  • Stratford General Hospital (ER)
  • Wellspring Stratford Cancer Centre

A GoFundMe launched with an initial goal of $4,500 and has now raised over $35,000. Every dollar is split evenly between these three organizations.

Simon: “People with cancer didn't choose this. That's what motivates me.”

Then people noticed

CTV, CBC, BlogTO, the Stratford Beacon Herald and a daily news podcast all picked it up, and the GoFundMe went from a $4,500 goal to over $35,000. That run is the reason this site exists.

A few things you might be wondering

How does a Shirtlab purchase help?

Every purchase at Shirtlab donates $1 to Canadians in need. This is Samm’s third charity project, separate from the run’s GoFundMe. Read the Shirtlab story.

Where does the run’s GoFundMe money go?

100% of the run’s GoFundMe funds are split evenly between three organizations: London Health Sciences Centre – Cancer Program, Stratford General Hospital (ER), and Wellspring Stratford Cancer Centre.

Why is the tone kept respectful?

This run is motivated by love and community. The content is written to raise funds and awareness without sensationalizing.

When was the run?

Samm completed the run on May 11, 2026.

Can I help without donating?

Yes. Share Samm’s Shirtlab project or the GoFundMe link, follow his updates, or introduce the projects to someone who might want to help.

Is this a registered charity?

No. It's Samm and the people who pitch in. Donations for the run go through GoFundMe, straight to the three organizations. This site doesn't take money and can't give you a tax receipt.

What's the African crypto charity initiative?

A separate effort, alongside the run, that raised almost $25,000 for people in need in Africa using $RENO, the Home Renovation Reviews community's coin on Solana. It's still going. Read about it.

How do I hear about the next one?

Samm posts on Home Renovation Reviews. You can also get the occasional email, or follow him on Facebook, TikTok and YouTube.
